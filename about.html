About Divinity Health & Fitness

Woombye's ONLY 24-Hour Gym • Comprehensive Health Services • Expert Team

Woombye's Premier Health & Fitness Destination

Welcome to Divinity Health and Fitness - Woombye's ONLY 24-hour gym! Located at 6-10 Blackall Street, we're proud to offer comprehensive health and fitness services to the Woombye community and beyond. Our unique facility provides round-the-clock access for our members, making fitness convenient for every schedule.

What sets us apart is our holistic approach to health and wellness. Beyond traditional gym facilities, we offer a full range of therapeutic services including massage therapy, DBT, osteopathy, and specialized self-defence classes. Our experienced team combines fitness expertise with therapeutic knowledge to support your complete wellness journey.

At Divinity Health & Fitness, we believe in empowering our community to unlock their best selves. Whether you're seeking personal training, therapeutic treatments, or simply want access to quality gym facilities any time of day or night, our dedicated team is here to support your goals with professionalism and care.
