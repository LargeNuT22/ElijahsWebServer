Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Simply fill out the contact form above or give me a call. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your goals and determine the best program for you.

Do you offer online training?

Yes! I offer virtual personal training sessions via video call, complete with custom workout programs and real-time form corrections.

What should I bring to my first session?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes, a water bottle, and a positive attitude! I'll provide all the necessary equipment.

How often should I train?

This depends on your goals and experience level. Typically, I recommend 2-3 sessions per week for optimal results, but we'll create a schedule that works for you.

Do you provide nutrition guidance?

Absolutely! Nutrition is a crucial part of any fitness journey. I offer comprehensive nutrition coaching as part of most programs or as a standalone service.

What's your cancellation policy?

I require 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. This allows me to potentially offer the slot to another client or adjust my schedule accordingly.
