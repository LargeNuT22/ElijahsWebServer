Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information

Last updated: 5 July 2026

1. Who we are

This website (divinity.fitness) is operated by Divinity Fitness Pty Ltd (ABN 99 657 001 799) trading as Divinity Health & Fitness ("we", "us", "our"), located at 6-10 Blackall Street, Woombye QLD 4559, Australia.

We respect your privacy and handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

2. What we collect through this website

When you use our contact form, we collect the details you choose to provide:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The service you are interested in
  • Anything you tell us about your fitness goals and preferred training times

Information about your fitness goals may include health-related information, which is treated as sensitive information under Australian privacy law. By submitting the form you consent to us collecting it for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. Please only include health details you are comfortable sharing — they are not required to make an enquiry.

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure also automatically logs limited technical information such as IP addresses and browser type for security and troubleshooting purposes.

3. Why we collect it

We collect this information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide the information you asked for
  • Arrange consultations, memberships or bookings you request
  • Maintain the security of our website

We do not send marketing emails or SMS. Contact details submitted through this website are used only to respond to you. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not run third-party tracking. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

5. Third-party services we rely on

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): our website is hosted on AWS in Australia.
  • EmailJS: contact form submissions are delivered to our business email using EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service. Your form details pass through EmailJS's systems for the sole purpose of delivering your message to us. See EmailJS's privacy policy for details.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google's servers, which means Google may receive standard technical data (such as your IP address) when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Social media links: our site links to our Instagram, Facebook and Google pages. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

6. CCTV at our premises

Our gym operates 24 hours a day and the premises are monitored by CCTV for the safety of members and the security of the facility. Footage is retained for a limited period, is accessed only in connection with security or safety incidents, and may be provided to police or as otherwise required by law.

7. How we store and protect your information

Contact form submissions are delivered to our business email inbox and handled only by the people who run the business. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask us at any time to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first and we will do our best to resolve it promptly. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page with its "last updated" date.
