Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the gym?

Simply fill out the contact form above or call us on 0455 558 278. We'll discuss membership options and arrange a tour of our facilities.

Where is the gym located?

We're at 6-10 Blackall Street in the heart of Woombye, with easy parking. We're less than 10 minutes' drive from Nambour and Palmwoods, making us a convenient 24 hour gym for the whole Sunshine Coast hinterland.

What makes you different from other gyms?

We're Woombye's ONLY 24-hour gym! Plus we offer comprehensive services including personal training, massage therapy, and self-defence classes.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes, a water bottle, and valid ID for membership signup. We provide all necessary gym equipment.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes! Our qualified personal trainers Ian Rook and Elijah Rook are available for one-on-one sessions to help you achieve your fitness goals.

What therapy services do you offer?

We offer massage therapy (with Ian Rook) alongside our fitness facilities.

How does 24-hour access work?

24-hour members receive secure keypad access to the gym at any time. Staff are available during business hours for assistance and appointments.
